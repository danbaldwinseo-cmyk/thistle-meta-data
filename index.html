1 of 4 — Company Name in Meta Titles

Section 1

The company name trade-off

Google truncates meta titles at approximately 65 characters. Every character in the company name is a character taken from the keyword or USP — the parts that drive rankings and click-through rates.

The spreadsheet presents two versions. The character count columns make the trade-off immediately visible.

Recommendation: use "Thistle Group" in the title. The full company name can then lead every meta description — appearing directly beneath the title in search results.

2 of 4 — Meta Descriptions

Section 2

The full brand name leads every description

Meta descriptions don't directly influence rankings, but they have a significant impact on click-through rate. Google allows up to 160 characters — considerably more room than the title.

The spreadsheet includes three description options per page:

Column M — Current
The existing live description, for comparison.
Column N — Proposed
Rewritten for keyword relevance, geographic targeting and clarity.
Column O — THI-Branded ✓ Recommended
Opens with "Thistle Home Improvements" as the very first words — designed to pair with the "Thistle Group" title.

How the combination appears in Google

thistle.group/bathrooms/
Bathrooms Aberdeen | Showroom, Design & Fitting | Thistle Group
Thistle Home Improvements bathroom showroom in Aberdeen. Expert bathroom design and fitting across Aberdeen and Aberdeenshire — finance options available.
thistle.group/plumbing-heating/
Plumbers Aberdeen | Plumbing & Heating Services | Thistle Group
Thistle Home Improvements — trusted local plumbers serving Aberdeen and Aberdeenshire. Bathroom fitting, boiler installation, heating and emergency call-outs.

The searcher sees "Thistle Group" in the title and "Thistle Home Improvements" as the first words they read in the snippet — the brand is prominent and unmistakable.

3 of 4 — Finance USPs

Section 3

Why some titles don't mention finance

The finance title option (Column C) is used across most pages. Where the titles in Columns A and C are identical, there is a specific reason — a stronger keyword or USP takes priority.

Product / Accreditation USP Stronger

Roofline — product terms ("Fascias, Soffits & Guttering") match what customers search for. Roofline is often a reactive purchase.

VELUX Roof Windows — "Certified Installers" is a stronger trust signal for a premium brand.

Electrical — "NICEIC Approved" is the primary trust signal for electrical work.

Plumbing & Heating — often an urgent or reactive purchase where finance is not a key driver.

Sale Price USP Stronger

Ex-Display Kitchens & Ex-Display Bathrooms — the entire value proposition is discounted pricing. Introducing finance alongside "Sale Prices" would dilute the core offer.

Brand Collection Pages

Beeck, Kuhlmann & Mobilturi — the brand name is the keyword. Adding a finance message would require removing it, which would undermine the purpose of the page entirely.

Product Terms Fill the Space

Outdoor Living Range — decking, fencing and garden rooms pages use material or product qualifiers ("uPVC & Composite", "Low-Maintenance", "No Painting Required") which are more specific and more searchable than a finance message.

4 of 4 — Our Recommendation

Section 4

Our recommendation

Use the finance title options (Column C) with "Thistle Group" as the company name, paired with the THI-branded meta descriptions (Column O) that open with "Thistle Home Improvements".

✓ Character-safe titles
All titles remain within 65 characters. Keyword, location and USP are fully visible — nothing is truncated.
✓ Strong CTR signal
0% finance is a proven click-through driver on considered-purchase pages: windows, doors, kitchens, bathrooms, conservatories and garden rooms.
✓ Full brand name in every snippet
Every description opens with "Thistle Home Improvements" — the first thing a searcher reads beneath the title. Prominent and unmistakable.
✓ Consistency across the site
"Thistle Group" in all titles. "Thistle Home Improvements" leading all descriptions. A clear, repeatable pattern across every page.

Note: where the finance title is identical to the USP title (see Section 3), Columns A and C are the same. In these cases, use the Column A/C title as-is, paired with the THI-branded description from Column O.
